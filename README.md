# U.S. Natural Gas Market Analysis | 2026

**Python-Based Energy Market Research | EIA Data | Natural Gas Storage & Henry Hub Prices**

## Project Overview

This project examines the relationship between U.S. natural gas underground storage activity and Henry Hub spot prices using publicly available data from the U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA).

The analysis focuses on weekly natural gas storage changes in the East Region, price volatility, seasonal market behavior, and the effects of extreme winter weather.

## Key Findings

- **39 weekly observations** were matched with Henry Hub prices for the 2026 analysis.
- **Correlation:** -0.608 between weekly East Region storage changes and Henry Hub prices.
- **Linear regression R²:** 0.3697, indicating that approximately 37% of observed price variation is explained by storage changes in this simple model.
- **Winter Storm Fern:** Extreme storage withdrawals coincided with significant natural gas price increases.
- **Sensitivity analysis:** Excluding two storm-related observations reduced the correlation magnitude to -0.539.

These findings indicate an inverse statistical relationship between storage changes and natural gas prices within the analyzed period. The relationship is not necessarily causal and may also reflect seasonal demand, weather conditions, and other market factors.
